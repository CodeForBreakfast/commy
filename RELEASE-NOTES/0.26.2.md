commy plugin: release 0.26.2

Patch release of the commy plugin, **0.26.1 → 0.26.2**.

This release tells a seat to wait on a reply through its topic subscription
instead of polling for it.

## Highlights

**The server instructions tell a seat to let its topic subscription wake it.**
The instructions said that posting or reacting in a topic subscribes the seat
to it, but not what to do with that, so a seat could still sleep and re-read
the thread to wait on a reply. They now tell the seat to let the reply wake
it, and to poll only what no subscription can wake it for.

Nothing.
